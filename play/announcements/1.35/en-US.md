## Drag the map to look around

Grab the map and drag it to see what is going on elsewhere, without moving your Woka. The camera comes back to your
Woka as soon as it walks. On a touch screen, drag with one finger and tap where you want to go.

![Dragging the map to look around](drag-the-map.mp4)

## A desktop app (beta)

When someone walks up to you while you work in another application, a small window pops up with the conversation,
over any app and on any screen. You see right away who is there, and you can answer without looking for the right tab.
[Download it](https://workadventu.re/download/) for Windows, macOS or Linux.

![The mini window of the desktop app, over a presentation](mini-window.webp)
