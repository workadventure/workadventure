# Finding people: the user list

The user list shows everyone in your WorkAdventure world, on this map or another one. From it, you can walk up to someone, find them on the map, join them on their map, or send them a message.

## Opening the user list

Click the people icon in the action bar, at the top-left of the screen, or press **U**. The list opens in the chat panel, under the title **Users**, with the number of people online.

![The user list: David and Gregory on this map, Grégoire on another map](images/user-list-panel.png)

People are grouped by map:

- **Is on this map**: the people on your map, you first. This section is open.
- one section per other map, named after the map (or its address). These sections are folded: click a section title to open or fold it.
- **Not connected to the world**: the members who are not connected right now. This section only appears if your administrator turned it on.

Each line shows the person's name, their [status](/user/availability-status), and **Admin** for administrators.

To find someone, open the search with the magnifying glass in the header and type part of their name.

Click a person's name to open their card, with the same actions as below.

## Actions on a person

Click **…** next to a person to see what you can do. The actions depend on where the person is.

### Someone on your map

![The actions on Gregory, who is on the same map](images/user-list-actions-here.png)

- **Talk To**: your Woka walks up to the person, and a [discussion bubble](/user/proximity-bubble) opens when you arrive.
- **Locate**: the view moves to the person and follows them, and their card opens. Close the card, or move, to come back to your Woka. If the person has left, a message says so.
- **Invite**: invites the person to join your conversation (see [Meeting invitations](/user/chat#meeting-invitations)).
- **Moderate**: block or report the person. Administrators can also kick or ban them.

![Locate: the view follows Gregory and his card opens](images/user-list-locate.png)

### Someone on another map

![The actions on Grégoire, who is on another map](images/user-list-actions-other.png)

- **Teleport**: takes you to the person's map, then your Woka walks up to them.
- **Invite** and **Moderate**, as above.

If the person has a business card, **Business Card** opens it.

## Sending a message

The paper plane icon next to a person opens a direct conversation with them in the chat. It only appears when the chat rooms are enabled in your world. It is greyed out for people who are not logged in.

## The Invite button

The **Invite** button at the bottom of the list does not invite a specific person: it opens the link of the room, to share it with someone who is not in WorkAdventure yet.
