# Invite People to your Map
If you want to share a link to other people to let them join your map, you can use the invite feature.
 In order to do that, you must follow those steps :
1. Click **Share**.
2. To choose where people arrive, turn on **Use a different entry point**.
3. Select an entry point. The list shows the start areas of the map: you can create them with the [map editor](inline-editor/area-editor/entry-exit.md).
4. Click **Copy**, then share the link.

Before copying, you can also turn on **Walk automatically to my position**: the link then makes people walk to where
you stand when you create the link. Moving afterwards does not update a link you have already shared.
